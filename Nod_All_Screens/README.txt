LOCKED PRODUCT DIRECTION
------------------------
Nod is currently a local-first Android networking and sharing app for exchanging:
- the user's OWN Nod identity / digital business card;
- optional Quick, Personal, Professional or custom share profiles;
- files between nearby devices.

Nod is NOT currently a general contact manager and should not browse, select or share
other contacts stored on the user's phone.

The black Nod wordmark with the lemon-green circular accent is the locked logo.
